# Draft variants for the X-fill interpretation

Both variants fix the confirmed defect of the first attempt (4 of 6 legs
were disconnected - the open quarters had removed the material the legs
attach to). Both are verified single-body solids.

- variant A: X-web floor under the four DEEP pockets only (30 mm deep,
  5 mm floor); the two shallow end pockets (15 mm) have a solid floor.
  Open quarters go through the 5 mm floor to the bottom face.
- variant B: every pocket has a solid floor at its depth, with an X-shaped
  ridge (4 mm wide, 3 mm tall) rising from the floor inside the pocket -
  a semi-open cradle.

Pick A or B (or describe corrections); then the model, tests and
verification report get finalized against the chosen variant.
